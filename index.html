🔍 The Gap Phenomenon Paradox

  • The Paradox: An impulse at a shorter coupling interval ($S_2 = 280\text{ ms}$) conducts, whereas an impulse at a longer interval ($S_2 = 340\text{ ms}$) blocks distally!
  • Electrophysiological Cause: At $340\text{ ms}$, $S_2$ traverses the AV node rapidly and strikes the distal bundle branch while it is still in absolute refractoriness $\rightarrow$ Distal Block.
  • Proximal Delay Brake: At $280\text{ ms}$, $S_2$ incurs marked delay in the proximal AV node. This delay grants the distal bundle branch enough time to recover, allowing $S_2$ to conduct!

⚡ R-on-T Vulnerability & VF Engine

  • Vulnerable Window: Occurs during the relative refractory period (T-wave peak-to-end, $\approx 180\text{--}280\text{ ms}$).
  • Re-entry Trigger: A PVC striking this window encounters inhomogeneous repolarization, causing rapid chaotic re-entry (Polymorphic VT / VF).
